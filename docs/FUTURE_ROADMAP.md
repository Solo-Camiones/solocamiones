# Future Roadmap

Post-MVP possibilities only. These items are shallow placeholders, not committed scope, and each requires separate validation and prioritization after MVP acceptance.

`FEATURES/15_ACCOUNTS_PAYABLE_PENDING_VALIDATION.md` is a new owner-priority proposal and is not automatically part of this roadmap or the confirmed MVP. If a **basic standalone payable ledger** is validated, only that narrow capability moves into phased delivery; supplier purchasing workflows, imports, landed cost, and accounting integration below remain future scope.

Dismantling and Installation Work Orders, mobile Mechanic claiming/completion, BEFORE/AFTER evidence, hierarchy synchronization, sale-linked Work Order create-or-reuse, and basic Administration and Operational Recovery are MVP core capabilities. They are intentionally not deferred to this roadmap.

## Operational Improvements

- Structured warehouse, zone, aisle, rack, and position locations.
- QR/barcode labels and scan-assisted handling.
- Guided physical counts, adjustments, and discrepancy review.
- Expanded operational, inventory, sales, payment, and management reporting.
- Controlled bulk import and bulk editing.
- Advanced workshop scheduling, prioritization, and workload planning beyond the MVP Work Order queue.

## Import and Cost Expansion

- Supplier records and purchasing workflows.
- Import, container, shipment, and related document tracking.
- Freight, customs, crane, transport, and other import charges.
- Landed-cost allocation across items and assemblies.
- Estimated-versus-actual import costs and exchange-rate handling for purchasing, import, and landed-cost work. The MVP's narrow `USD`-invoice profitability conversion is not this capability.
- Advanced profit analysis by item, assembly, supplier, shipment, or container.

## Fiscal and Accounting

- DGII integration and validated fiscal-compliance workflows.
- NCF and e-CF issuance.
- Fiscal credit notes and required electronic documents.
- Fiscal reports and accounting-system integration.

## Advanced Commercial and Service Operations

- Advanced reservation expiration, queues, deposits, extensions, and approvals.
- Advanced returns, exchanges, inspection-driven restoration, and customer-return logistics.
- Warranty cases and claim history.
- Accounts receivable aging, collections workflows, interest, installment plans, automated reminders, and bank reconciliation. Customer credit limits/terms, `ABONADO` payment labels, Administrator-only CxC, and account-statement PDF are in the confirmed pre-production change set (Features 08/12), not this roadmap.
- Advanced approval rules and configurable operational permissions.
- Advanced recovery automation and cross-system support tooling beyond the MVP's named recovery operations and consistency diagnostics.

## Product Differentiation

- Interactive 3D assembly visualization and component-state overlays.
- Vehicle, engine, and part compatibility data or integrations.
- Image-assisted part, plate, or identifier recognition.
- Customer self-service portal.
- Ecommerce catalog, ordering, and fulfillment integration.

## Possible Future SaaS

- Multi-tenant company isolation.
- Guided company onboarding and per-company configuration.
- Plans, feature limits, and usage limits.
- Subscription management and SaaS billing.
- Central platform administration and tenant support tools.
